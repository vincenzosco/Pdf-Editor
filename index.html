How it works

Three steps, no account

Open the editor, pick a tool, and get your file back. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

01

Open your PDF

Drop a file into the editor. It is read in the browser, so editing it never uploads it.

02

Click the text you want to change

The page renders with its real text on top: click a word and type over it, keeping its size and font.

03

Export the result

Download the edited PDF. The file on your device stays untouched.

Workflow

Annotations that look like they were made on paper

Every highlight, underline, and margin note renders at full resolution. Export with annotations baked in or send a clean copy — your choice.

  • Layer-based — annotations sit on their own layer until you're ready to flatten
  • Consistent — the same result in every browser, with no plugin and no install
  • Export ready — PDF/A, flattened, or annotated — whichever your recipient needs
